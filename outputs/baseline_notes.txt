======================================================================

  scale_pos_weight), which deliberately shifts predicted probabilities
  upward to favor recall on the minority class. Brier score punishes
  that distortion. Do NOT compare these Brier values against future
  unweighted or calibrated models without re-reading this note.
  Ranking metrics (ROC-AUC, PR-AUC) are unaffected by the weighting.
