CORRECTION — 2026-08-25
======================================================================
  The slice section above previously read:

      "The 21 was_missing flag columns can never fire at test time —
       they are train-era artifacts."

  That figure was wrong twice over, and both errors are corrected above.

  The count. There are 22 was_missing columns in the feature matrix, not
  21: 13 from the bureau block collected from around December 2015, 8
  from the mths_since_* family, and emp_length_was_missing. No grouping
  of the matrix produces 21.

  The sweep. The claim generalised from one column, open_acc_6m, whose
  flag is indeed constant zero in 2017, to every was_missing column.
  Only 11 of the 22 are constant zero there. The other 11 do fire, some
  of them heavily: mths_since_last_record_was_missing on 80.5% of test
  rows, and il_util_was_missing on 13.5% despite belonging to the same
  December-2015 bureau block that motivated the original claim. Calling
  the whole family "dead weight at test time" was not supported.

  src/07_audit.py has been changed to count these from the matrix rather
  than state a fixed number, so the figure cannot go stale again. Note
  that regenerating this file will reproduce the corrected counts but not
  this correction block, which is written by hand.

  The same "21" figure also appears in README.md, which is being rewritten
  separately and has not been edited here.
